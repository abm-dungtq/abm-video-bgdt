## Video direction

- **Preset:** none. This is a hand-written design truth in `frame.md` ("Tia lửa"), chosen by the user on 2026-09-25 so the lesson looks different from the Hermes one.
- **Concept:** "Tia lửa" (the spark). Every idea lights up with a small terracotta ✳ on the spoken word, like a margin note in a warm notebook. Claude is shown as a calm, capable colleague, not a flashy machine.
- **Recurring motif:**
  - the ✳ spark, which pops on the hero keyword and can travel along a hand-drawn path;
  - the hand-drawn terracotta stroke, used for underlines, circles and arrows.
  - Both appear at least once per chapter.
- **Palette roles:**
  - canvas #1F1E1D and surface #2A2926 cards;
  - cream #F0EEE6 for all text;
  - terracotta #D97757 as the single accent per shot;
  - sage #8FB8A8 for ok/connected states and the terminal prompt;
  - muted #9A968C for secondary labels;
  - red #E5484D only in frame 41 and chapter 7.
- **Chapter glow:** the ground radial uses hue 22 + 3 × chapter, at 55 % saturation and 0.20 alpha, so every chapter stays warm. Surfaces and accents never shift.
- **Motion grammar:** reveals land on the spoken cue with power3.out (0.4–0.7 s), moves use power2.inOut, and draw-ons tied to speech are linear. There is no bounce and no CSS 3D. Every shot change visibly changes the layout, scale or subject.
- **Held beats:** each chapter holds still for at least 2 s right after its densest reveal. The drafts name the exact frame.
- **Analyze rail:** frame 44 builds the three slots ① Tính năng · ② Vì sao nổi bật · ③ Bạn được gì. The rail stays small at y 64–108 in the ch5–ch6 content frames, and each slot lights on its `rail:` cue.
- **Real screenshots:** these are the approved, redacted claude.ai captures in `assets/screens/`, named by frame number.
  - They sit in a surface viewport, and zoom is at most 1.4×.
  - Terracotta callouts appear on the cue, and pixels are never altered.
  - Frame 66 is a labeled "MINH HỌA" mock.
- **Layout safe area:**
  - Content stays inside x 80–1840, y 60–880.
  - The chapter label sits at x 40–520, y 14–50, and the progress bar in the top 10 px.
  - The karaoke band is y ≥ 918.
  - Rail frames start their content at y ≥ 140.
- **Terminal truth rule:** terminal shots show only text from `capture/terminal/` (`claude --version`, `claude --help`).
- **Negative list:**
  - no logos: Anthropic, Claude, Slack and the rest appear as text chips only;
  - no purple-blue "AI" gradients, no bokeh or orbs;
  - no fake cursors or browser chrome outside the approved screenshots and the labeled mock;
  - no invented UI, numbers or commands;
  - red only where the content is a warning.
