## Video direction

- **Preset:** `code-editorial` (via `build-frame.mjs`, remixed and inverted onto the dark Messenger palette). Reason: it is the only candidate with a built-in code/mono voice and hairline editorial restraint, and it suits a dark, technical lesson with terminal replays. `cobalt-grid` is a single-ink graph paper, and `cartesian` has no code register.
- **Concept "Sứ giả" (the messenger):** Hermes is the messenger of the gods, and the agent is a courier that carries your task out and brings the result back. Every diagram shows work travelling somewhere and coming back done.
- **Trail motif:** a thin glowing gold (`#FFD23F`) path with a small winged spark at its head. It draws itself across every transition seam, connects nodes in hubs and flows, walks stations in journeys, and writes the numeral on every chapter card (`title` shots). Registry references: `svg-stroke-trace` (the path), `offset-path-traveler` (the spark), `tracing-beam` (connector lighting), `constellation-hub`, `code-terminal-run`, `count-up` / `conic-progress-ring`, `grid-card-assemble`, `success-check` / `state-chip-rail`, `pull-back-reveal` / `ui-focus-zoom`, `pan-stations`. Frame workers adapt their motion; nothing is mounted verbatim.
- **Palette roles:** canvas `#0B1026` (midnight indigo) · surface `#141B3A` (cards, terminal panels) · ink `#F4F1E8` · accent gold `#FFD23F` (trail, the one hero keyword per shot, ticks) · accent-2 cyan `#3EE6D0` (ok/ready states, secondary connectors) · warn coral `#FF6B6B` (chapter 7 only: blocked/dangerous). Type: Be Vietnam Pro 800 display / 500 body, JetBrains Mono for terminal, commands, file names and kickers.
- **Chapter hue shift:** the background radial gradient tilts +12° hue per chapter (ch0 0°, ch1 +12°, … ch7 +84°) around the indigo canvas. Surfaces and accents never shift. The shift lands on each chapter's `title` shot.
- **Motion grammar:** long-tail `power3` settles, smooth rather than bouncy. Each shot reveals on its spoken cue (keyword tokens in `script.json` get a key-text reveal at their word start; phase 5 wires aligned times). Nothing enters before the VO reaches it, and the back half of each shot carries reveals. During holds, a subtle jitter at most; no lazy breathing and no back-half pan or push. Internal shot seams are velocity-matched cuts or trail wipes. Only frame 63 has a real exit (the spark flies off).
- **Held beats (≥2 s still read, placed after the densest reveal):** ch0 frame 3 (the "0" telemetry lands) · ch1 frame 8 ("tự sửa sai") · ch2 frame 15 ("người mới ✓") · ch3 frame 27 (the safety/focus meters) · ch4 frame 31 (only the important notebook lines stay lit) · ch5 frame 45 (the chained jobs) · ch6 frame 56 (the triple beat before the recap) · ch7 frame 59 (the blocklist vault). Recap `anchor` frames at chapter ends also resolve still.
- **Chapter 6 analyze pattern:** frame 48 creates a three-slot rail ("① Tính năng · ② Vì sao nổi bật · ③ Bạn được gì") that sits small at the top of frames 49–55. Each slot lights as a separate cued reveal, in order.
- **Layout:** all content sits between y 10 and 880 px. The top 10 px is the progress bar; y ≥ 918 is the 162 px karaoke band. A centered hero anchors near y ≈ 454. The hero fills 40–60% of the frame. Framings rotate: centered, split 50/50, asymmetric 60/40, triptych, full-width strip, ring. The same framing is never used twice in a row.
- **Terminal truth:** `terminal` shots replay only real text: `capture/terminal/*.txt` or commands quoted in `capture/extracted/visible-text.txt` (install one-liners, `hermes model`, `hermes tools`, `/tools disable browser`, `hermes --continue`, `hermes journey`, `hermes gateway setup|start`, `hermes mcp`). No invented output.
- **Negative list:** no logos (Hermes, Nous, or platform brand marks; platform names are shown as text chips), no purple-blue "AI" gradients, no floating bokeh, no real cursors or browser chrome, no coral outside chapter 7. Avoid both failure modes: the slideshow (front-load, then freeze) and the screensaver (everything floating independently). No SFX and no music (`sfx: none`).
